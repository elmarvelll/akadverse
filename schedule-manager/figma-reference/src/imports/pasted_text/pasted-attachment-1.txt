🏫 SCHOOL ADMIN PROFILE (UPDATED)
🎯 DESIGN GOAL

The admin profile should feel like:

a command center

focused on oversight, control, and insights

clean, powerful, and not cluttered

🔷 STRUCTURE
1. PROFILE HEADER

Contains:

Admin Name

Role (e.g. Academic Admin, Registrar, Dean)

Department / Office

Profile Image

Optional:

Admin badge (e.g. “School Admin”)

2. LEFT PANEL (ADMIN SUMMARY CARD)

This is the quick control snapshot.

Include:

Total Students

Total Faculty

Active Courses

Pending Approvals 🔥 (highlight this — Von Restorff)

Animation

Numbers count up on load

“Pending Approvals” has a subtle pulse/glow

3. RIGHT PANEL (MAIN CONTENT)
🔹 SECTION A — ACADEMIC OVERVIEW

This is the core insight area.

Include:

Total courses running

Department performance overview

Average GPA across school

Attendance trends

Visuals:

Bar charts (department performance)

Line chart (attendance trend)

Animation:

Charts draw in smoothly

Data fades in

🔹 SECTION B — STUDENT MANAGEMENT

Focus on student monitoring.

Include:

Students with low attendance

Students with pending fees (optional)

Students at academic risk

Interaction:

Each item is clickable → opens detailed list

🔹 SECTION C — FACULTY MANAGEMENT

Include:

Faculty attendance compliance

Classes without uploaded attendance

Pending grading submissions

🔹 SECTION D — APPROVALS & ACTIONS 🔥

This is the most important section.

Include:

Course approvals

Marketplace approvals (if needed later)

Faculty submissions

Requests

Design:

Use cards or list items with action buttons:

[ New Course Request ]
Approve | Reject
Animation

Cards slide in

Buttons have ripple effect

Pending items glow slightly

🔹 SECTION E — REPORTS & INSIGHTS

Include:

Downloadable reports

Academic summaries

Attendance reports

Performance analytics

🔹 SECTION F — ANNOUNCEMENTS / CONTROL

Admin can:

Post announcements

Send messages to students/faculty

✨ ANIMATIONS (ADMIN SPECIFIC)

Metrics → count-up animation

Alerts → subtle pulse

Charts → draw animation

Cards → lift on hover

Actions → ripple on click

⚠️ IMPORTANT UX ADJUSTMENTS
Use Hick’s Law

Do NOT overload admin with everything at once.

Group into sections:

Overview
Students
Faculty
Approvals
Reports
Use Von Restorff

Highlight:

👉 Pending Approvals
👉 Students at Risk

These should stand out visually.
